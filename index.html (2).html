Nallveerappa Mudaliyar

The Mudaliyar is an honorific title of nobility and leadership traditionally held among distinguished Tamil communities. Our family lineage traces to this respected ancestral name, preserved across generations.

Chittur Sri Meenakshi Sundareshvara Temple

This sacred temple dedicated to Lord Shiva (Sundareshvara) and Goddess Sri Meenakshi was built by our forefathers approximately 1,800 years ago. It stands as a living testament to our ancestral devotion and architectural legacy.
